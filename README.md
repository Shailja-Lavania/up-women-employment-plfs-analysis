# Women's Employment in Uttar Pradesh

This repository contains a descriptive R-based analysis of women’s employment in Uttar Pradesh using the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) 2023–24.

## Research focus

The analysis examines employment patterns among female workers in Uttar Pradesh, with particular attention to the distinction between employment participation and the type of work women undertake.

## Main finding

PLFS 2023–24 Table 19 shows that among rural female workers in Uttar Pradesh:

- 48.9% were helpers in household enterprises.
- 41.1% were own-account workers or employers.
- 7.1% were casual labourers.
- 2.9% were regular wage or salaried workers.

These figures describe the distribution of employment categories among rural female workers. They do not measure income, job quality, social security, control over earnings, or the impact of a particular welfare scheme.

## Files

- `female_employment_types.R` creates the chart of employment categories among rural female workers.
- `PLFS2023-24.R` contains the initial analysis of worker-population ratios among young women.
- `rural_female_employment_types.png` is the generated chart.
- `PLFSanalysis.Rproj` opens the project in RStudio.

## Data source

Periodic Labour Force Survey Annual Report 2023–24, Table 19: Percentage distribution of workers in usual status (principal status plus subsidiary status) by broad employment status for each State and Union Territory.

## Reproducing the chart

1. Open `PLFSanalysis.Rproj` in RStudio.
2. Open `female_employment_types.R`.
3. Click Source to run the full script.
4. The chart is generated and saved locally.
